## Zad.1
Samochod przejechal droge 15km w 10 minut ze stala predkoscia. Obliczyc predkosc samochodu.
${}s = 15km{}$
${}t = 15min{}$
${}v = ?{}$

${}v = \frac{s}{t}{}$
${}t = 10min = \frac{10}{60}h = \frac{1}{6}h{}$
${}v = \frac{15km}{\frac{1}{6}h} = 90km/h{}$

## Zad.3
Z Torunia do Bydgoszczy wyjechal pociag ze stala predkoscia 90km/h. W tym samym momencie wyjechal rowniez pociag z bydgoszczy do torunia z taka sama predkoscia. Po jakim czasie mina sie te pociagi?

${}v = 90km/h{}$
${}s = 45km{}$
${}t = ?{}$

${}t * 90 \frac{km}{h} = 45km - 90 \frac{km}{h} * t{}$
${}180 \frac{km}{h} * t = 45km{}$
${}4 \frac{km}{h} * t = 1km{}$
${}t = 1km * \frac{1}{4} \frac{km}{h} = \frac{1}{4}h = 15min{}$

### Alternatywny sposob
${}s_{1} = vt{}$
${}s_{2} = vt{}$
${}s = s_{1} + s_{2}{}$
${}s = v_{1}t + v_{2}t{}$
${}s=t(v_{1}+v_{2}){}$
${}t = \frac{s}{v_{1}+v_{2}}{}$

${}t = \frac{45km}{90 \frac{km}{h} + 90 \frac{km}{h}} = \frac{45km}{180 \frac{km}{h}} = \frac{1}{4}h = 15min{}$


## Zad.4
Kierowca formuly 1 przez pierwsze 5 minut jechal z predkoscia 240km/h. Po drobnej kolizji zmniejszyl predkosc do 100 km/h i jechal tak przez 10 min. Jaka byla jego srednia predkosc na tej trasie?

${}t_{1} = 5 min, v_{1} = 240 \frac{km}{h}{}$
${}t_{2} = 10min, v_{2} = 100 \frac{km}{h}{}$
${}v_{sr}=?{}$

${}v_{sr} = \frac{s_{c}}{t_{c}}{}$
${}s_{c} = s_{1} + s_{2}{}$
${}t_{c} = t_{1}+t_{2}{}$

${}t_{c} = 5min + 10min = 15min = \frac{1}{4}h{}$

${}s_{1} = \frac{1}{30}h * 240 \frac{km}{h} = 8km{}$
${}s_{2} = \frac{1}{6}h * 100 \frac{km}{h} = 16 \frac{2}{3}{km}{}$
${}s_{c} = 8km + 16 \frac{2}{3}km = 36,67km{}$

${}v_{sr} = \frac{36,67km}{\frac{1}{4}h} = 147 \frac{km}{h}{}$
